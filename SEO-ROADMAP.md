## Internal linking strategy

1. Every SAFE cluster page ↔ `/safe-certification-immobiliere`
2. Every buying guide ↔ documents + promoteur + risques + livraison
3. City articles ↔ `/villes` + prix Alger when relevant
4. Homepage → featured article + pillars + SAFE block
5. Category pages → related SEO cluster links
6. Multilingual: hreflang on all translated equivalents; x-default = FR
