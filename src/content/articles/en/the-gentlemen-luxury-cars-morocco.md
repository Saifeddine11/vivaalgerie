## What Accrocar confirms

Accrocar is a Morocco-based luxury-car rental and automotive concierge business. For The Gentlemen, it says it supported production vehicle requirements and worked directly with **Dune Films** and **MOONAGE PICTURES LIMITED**. The statement was provided directly by Accrocar to the publisher, making it a first-party source for the company’s own activity.

The wording matters. It supports “supplied luxury vehicles for production requirements” and “collaborated directly with Dune Films and Moonage.” It does not disclose purchase orders, invoices, driver lists or the complete procurement chain. It also does not prove a direct Netflix contract. A series supplier may be engaged through the production company, a local service producer, a transport department or another authorised production channel.

## Netflix, Moonage Pictures and the production layer

[Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/the-gentlemen-season-3-release-date-cast-news) confirms that Season 3 is filming in the UK and Morocco. Netflix is the platform and commissioning/distribution context. [Moonage Pictures](https://cms.moonagepictures.com/the-gentlemen/) identifies The Gentlemen as a Moonage production for Netflix in association with Miramax Television.

The UK corporate register lists **MOONAGE PICTURES (THE GENTLEMEN) LIMITED**, company number 14115797, as an active television programme production business. The shorter “MOONAGE PICTURES LIMITED” wording appears in Accrocar’s confirmation; the [Companies House record](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/14115797) is the precise reference when a legal entity name is required.

The register proves the company’s existence and declared activity. It does not publish Morocco vendor agreements. Accrocar’s statement addresses its own collaboration. Keeping those sources separate avoids making one document claim more than it does.

## Dune Films and local production services

[Dune Films](https://dunefilms.com/) presents itself as a full-service Moroccan production company established in 1998, with Marrakech and Ouarzazate bases. Its public capabilities include locations, permits, crews, equipment, transport, fleets and local coordination. That is the service layer that connects an international production’s requirements with Morocco’s practical environment.

Dune Films is not Moonage Pictures, and Accrocar is not Dune Films. Moonage produces the series; Dune provides Moroccan production services; Accrocar contributes specialist vehicle supply. Netflix remains a distinct commissioning and distribution entity.

## What production luxury-car supply actually involves

A production booking is not simply a tourist rental with a camera nearby. The brief may specify dates, cities, visual role, driver, call time, duration and surface conditions. The supplier needs to confirm that the actual vehicle is available, prepare it consistently, establish insurance responsibilities and organise delivery and collection around the unit schedule.

A car may be a picture vehicle, talent transport, executive transport or another production requirement. Accrocar confirms luxury-vehicle supply but does not publicly assign each supplied vehicle to one of those categories. “Supplied to the production” should therefore not be automatically rewritten as “seen on screen.”

Condition continuity becomes important when a vehicle is photographed. Bodywork, cabin, wheels and cleanliness must match between takes. Dust or rain may require repeated preparation. The production needs an authorised driver, a handover record and a plan for late wraps or location changes. Moving between Rabat and a pre-Saharan area introduces longer distances, heat, dust and less immediate technical support.

Accrocar’s [public production-hire guide](https://accrocar.com/blog/luxury-car-rental-film-production-morocco) describes its general B2B process: selecting from a real fleet, matching call-sheet dates, confirming locations, setting driver or chauffeur responsibilities, preparing the vehicle, documenting insurance and issuing a written quote. The guide says Accrocar does not ordinarily publish named clients or productions.

## Why cars matter to The Gentlemen’s visual identity

The Gentlemen uses property, clothing, interiors and cars to communicate hierarchy and power. A vehicle can introduce a character before dialogue, turn an arrival into a statement or contrast cultivated wealth with criminal risk. That visual importance makes preparation and continuity part of the creative work, not merely transport administration.

It is reasonable to explain why Rolls-Royce or Bentley types fit this world. It is not reasonable to attach one to the Moroccan unit without evidence. Editorial analysis and production fact occupy different columns, and both are more useful when labelled correctly.

## Production vehicles across Morocco

Rabat and Erfoud were reported as Moroccan locations, while Netflix confirms Morocco at country level. Urban and pre-Saharan environments impose different constraints. Rabat requires coordination with traffic, access, parking and public space. A move toward Erfoud adds distance, dust, temperature and reduced proximity to maintenance.

A luxury vehicle may need covered storage, repeated cleaning, controlled drivers and careful route selection. Low vehicles, wide SUVs and highly finished bodywork do not react identically to medina edges, unpaved access or long transfers. The production supplier’s value lies in turning these constraints into a documented plan rather than improvising on the call day.

## What this confirmation demonstrates

The Accrocar case shows that Morocco’s screen economy includes specialised local vendors, not only landscapes and studio space. International productions buy coordination, reliability, documentation and contingency. The local services company connects global requirements to Moroccan crews and rules; the automotive specialist solves a narrow but visible production problem.

For Accrocar, the work is evidence of B2B production capability. For Dune Films, it sits within broader local services. For Moonage, it is one part of organising a multi-country series. These conclusions do not imply shared ownership, exclusivity or a direct Netflix appointment.

## FAQ

### Did Accrocar work on The Gentlemen in Morocco?

Yes, according to its direct confirmation. Accrocar says it worked with Dune Films and MOONAGE PICTURES LIMITED and supplied luxury vehicles for production requirements.

### Is Accrocar an official Netflix supplier?

That exact claim is not established. The confirmed statement concerns the Morocco production and direct collaboration with Dune Films and Moonage, not a published direct Netflix appointment.

### Which luxury cars appear in Season 3?

No Morocco-supplied model has been publicly confirmed as an on-screen car. Fleet images on this page demonstrate capability only.

### Are Dune Films and Moonage Pictures the same company?

No. Moonage Pictures is the series production company; Dune Films is a Moroccan production-services company.

### Does vehicle supply always mean a picture car?

No. Production vehicles can be used on screen, for talent or executive transport, or for another operational requirement. Accrocar has not publicly assigned each supplied vehicle to a category.
