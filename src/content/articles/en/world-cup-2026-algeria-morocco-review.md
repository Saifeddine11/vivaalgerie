> Editorial update: research completed on 21 July 2026 at 15:15 (Africa/Casablanca), after the 19 July final.

The 2026 World Cup is over: Spain beat Argentina 1-0 after extra time on 19 July, according to FIFA. For Algeria and Morocco, the review must start from official results: Algeria exited in the Round of 32 against Switzerland; Morocco stopped in the quarter-finals against France.

<div class="key-facts"><h2>Key points</h2><ul><li>Algeria: Argentina 3-0, Jordan 1-2 Algeria, Algeria 3-3 Austria, Switzerland 2-0 Algeria.</li><li>Morocco: Brazil 1-1, Scotland 0-1 Morocco, Morocco 4-2 Haiti, Netherlands 1-1 Morocco (2-3 pens), Canada 0-3 Morocco, France 2-0 Morocco.</li><li>Spain are 2026 world champions, Argentina runners-up and England third, according to FIFA.</li></ul></div>

## Algeria: useful return, early exit

Algeria were back at the World Cup for the first time since 2014. The official path shows a team capable of reacting against Jordan and Austria, but punished by Argentina and then Switzerland. The review is mixed: progress from the group phase, but a gap to close in knockout football.

## Morocco: continuity at African high level

Morocco confirmed a high level after 2022: second in the group, penalty shootout win over the Netherlands, 3-0 against Canada, then a 2-0 defeat to France. The quarter-final confirms continental consistency, even if the semi-final step was not reached this time.

## A Maghreb reading without easy rivalry

Comparing Algeria and Morocco should not turn football into national conflict. The two paths say different things: Algeria returned to the global stage; Morocco consolidated its rank. For more context, read our [analysis](/en/analysis), [news](/en/news), [blog](/en/blog).
