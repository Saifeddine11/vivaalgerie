> Editorial update: research completed on 21 July 2026 at 14:55 (Africa/Casablanca).

The fire at the assisted-childhood facility in Mohammadia, Algiers, requires factual, respectful and non-graphic coverage. Available official sources report 11 dead and 19 injured. The DGSN attributes the origin to an electrical spark from an air conditioner, while saying the investigation continues.

<div class="key-facts"><h2>Key points</h2><ul><li>The fire occurred on 16 July 2026 around 3:32 a.m., according to Civil Protection.</li><li>The official APS-cited toll is 11 dead and 19 injured.</li><li>No victim imagery is used in this article.</li></ul></div>

## What is established

Emergency services brought the fire under control and evacuated the injured to hospitals, including the Zeralda burns hospital according to APS. Authorities also mentioned care for children with specific needs. These facts are enough to inform without exposing victims or using copyrighted press images.

## The investigation and its limits

The DGSN statement cited by APS refers to an electrical spark from an air conditioner installed in a first-floor room. The wording must stay attributed: it is the forensic conclusion announced at this stage, not an invitation to speculate about individual responsibility before procedures are complete.

## Safety in vulnerable facilities

The issue goes beyond one building. During heatwaves, air conditioners run for long periods and electrical installations can be stressed. Facilities hosting children, elderly people or dependent publics need audits, maintenance, usable exits and repeated safety instructions.
